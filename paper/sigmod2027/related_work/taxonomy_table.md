# Taxonomy Table

| Category | Representative works | What prior work provides | What prior work lacks | How LAFC-Evict differs |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Classical cache replacement | Belady, LIRS, ARC, CLOCK-Pro, GreedyDual-Size, TinyLFU/W-TinyLFU | Strong algorithmic baselines and design intuitions for recency, frequency, adaptivity, and cost/size awareness | No public benchmark artifact with candidate-level supervised labels; usually no release-oriented task views | \lafc\ uses these policies as baselines or reference points, but focuses on labeled benchmark construction rather than proposing another replacement rule |
| Learned caching and ML-based eviction | RL-Cache, ALPS, Feedforward Neural Networks for Caching, HR-Cache | Learned or learning-assisted cache-management policies, often with policy-level evaluations | Usually optimize or compare policies directly; do not release per-candidate finite-horizon supervision datasets | \lafc\ is not a new learned policy; it exposes reusable supervision for multiple learning tasks |
| Cache traces and workload analysis | Twemcache analyses, large-scale key-value workload analysis, UMass Trace Repository | Real traces, workload characterization, and public trace infrastructure | Raw traces alone do not provide decision-aligned candidate labels or benchmark task definitions | \lafc\ sits above raw traces by releasing generated candidate-level labels and derived benchmark views |
| Systems benchmark packages | YCSB, OLTP-Bench, JOB | Reusable experimental workloads and benchmark interfaces for systems research | Not eviction-label benchmarks; no per-candidate counterfactual supervision | \lafc\ aims to play a similar benchmark-role for learned cache eviction specifically |
| Ranking and learning-to-rank benchmarks | LETOR, Yahoo! LTR Challenge, learning-to-rank survey | Public ranking datasets, pairwise/listwise task formulations, reusable evaluation culture | Not cache-decision datasets; do not model candidate evictions or finite-horizon cache loss | \lafc\ borrows ranking-task ideas but grounds them in cache-decision supervision |
| Counterfactual learning and offline evaluation | Counterfactual risk minimization, contextual-bandit OPE, unbiased learning-to-rank | Principles for learning and evaluating from partial or logged feedback | Not cache-specific; generally do not package cache-decision datasets with labeled candidate actions | \lafc\ imports these ideas into a cache-eviction benchmark with explicit candidate, decision, and pairwise views |
