\section{Empirical Characterization}
\label{sec:characterization}

The empirical center of gravity for this paper should be benchmark characterization, not an oversized model bake-off. The release metadata already supports several useful facts: total candidate rows, total decision rows, pairwise-sample size, split counts, family counts, capacity counts, and horizon counts. These are enough to establish the scale and heterogeneity of the benchmark in the current submission draft.

The present manuscript therefore limits characterization claims to metadata-backed, decision-view-backed, and pairwise-sample-backed summaries. Detailed row-level distributions of \texttt{y\_loss} and \texttt{y\_value} require a candidate-row scan and are intentionally omitted here rather than approximated from incomplete metadata.
