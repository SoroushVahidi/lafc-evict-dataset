\section{Empirical Characterization}
\label{sec:characterization}

The empirical center of gravity for this paper should be benchmark characterization, not an oversized model bake-off. The release metadata already supports several useful facts: total candidate rows, total decision rows, pairwise-sample size, split counts, family counts, capacity counts, and horizon counts. These are enough to establish the scale and heterogeneity of the benchmark in the first submission draft.

What remains missing are the candidate-row label-distribution summaries that make the benchmark scientifically interpretable at the row level. By contrast, decision-view-backed tie frequency, regret spread, candidate-count distributions per decision, and pairwise-sample balance can already be extracted without any candidate-row scan.
