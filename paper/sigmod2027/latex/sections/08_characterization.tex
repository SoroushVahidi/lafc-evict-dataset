\section{Empirical Characterization}
\label{sec:characterization}

The empirical characterization emphasizes benchmark structure rather than a large model bake-off. The release metadata establish the scale and heterogeneity of the benchmark, and the committed candidate-row summaries add full label-distribution reporting without changing that focus.

Across 277,995,072 candidate rows, \texttt{y\_loss} has mean 7.7561, standard deviation 4.6542, and 5/25/50/75/95th percentiles of 2/4/7/11/16. The corresponding \texttt{y\_value} summary mirrors those values by sign, as expected from the benchmark definition. The resulting label surface is broad but still bounded by the finite horizon, which keeps both candidate-level regression errors and decision-level regret interpretable.

The same committed result files also show that the simple linear-score selector often lands in the benchmark-optimal set even though its underlying value regression is weak. That combination is useful descriptive context for later benchmark users: the release is not trivial, but a lightweight heuristic already clears a reproducible lower bar.
