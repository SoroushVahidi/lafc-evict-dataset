\section*{Declaration of Generative AI and AI-assisted technologies in the writing process}

The authors used generative AI and AI-assisted coding and search tools to support manuscript drafting and editing, related-work exploration, code development, debugging, experiment organization, and result and table summarization. All scientific claims, related-work interpretations, code, experiments, tables, and final manuscript text were reviewed and verified by the authors, who remain fully responsible for the content and correctness of this work. No AI tool is listed as an author or as a contributor to authorship.
