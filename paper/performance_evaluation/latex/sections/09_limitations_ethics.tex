\section{Limitations, Ethics, and Reuse}
\label{sec:limitations}

The evaluations and claims in this paper are bounded by several explicit constraints:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
  \item \textbf{Target Scope:} The label is a finite-horizon counterfactual miss cost under a specified continuation policy, not an offline-optimal label for the full future control problem. The canonical release uses $H\in\{4,8,16\}$; the matched extended sensitivity check ($H\in\{16,32,64,128\}$) uses a separate simulation pool that does not alter the public release.
  \item \textbf{Continuation Policy:} Canonical labels assume LRU continuation. The sampled and full-population studies establish robustness only within their tested limits (MRU and mean-random); arbitrary continuation policies remain outside this claim.
  \item \textbf{Cache Abstraction:} The evaluated dataset assumes unit-object caching, count-based capacities (32--256), and unconditional admission. Variable sizes, byte-weighted costs, and admission-gated models are not covered.
  \item \textbf{Target Degeneracy:} The target is substantially tied (aggregate all-tied fraction $\approx$0.68), heavily influenced by trace-level reuse structures (Section~\ref{sec:characterization}).
  \item \textbf{Family Caveats:} As noted in Section~\ref{sec:benchmark-design}, metacdn's closed-loop evidence relies on a validation-window split, and metakv is evaluated from a cold start.
  \item \textbf{Feature Schema:} A forensic audit found 19 of the 26 historical candidate-level feature columns to be constant or redundant; only 7 carry independent nonconstant variation. No claim relies on the deprecated fields being informative.
  \item \textbf{Governance and Provenance:} Reuse must preserve the attribution and licensing constraints of upstream traces. While all five evaluated families are publicly available in the Hugging Face \texttt{v1.0} release, hosting remains asymmetric across platforms: AWS remains v0.3/wiki2018-only and Zenodo remains v0.2.
  \item \textbf{Learned-Policy Bounds:} The frozen learned-policy check (Section~\ref{sec:learned-closed-loop}) covers only one model class, target, and feature set. It demonstrates offline-to-online divergence for that specific configuration, but does not serve as an exhaustive study of learned architectures or represent a ceiling on learned-eviction performance.
\end{itemize}
