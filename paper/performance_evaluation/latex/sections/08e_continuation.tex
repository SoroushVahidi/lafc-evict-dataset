\section{Continuation-Policy Sensitivity (RQ4)}
\label{sec:continuation}

The canonical labels (Section~\ref{sec:label-generation}) use LRU as the
continuation policy after a forced eviction. This choice matters because a
deployed learned policy would control later evictions, not necessarily
LRU. RQ4 therefore asks whether the optimal-candidate set and regret
ordering induced by the canonical labels are materially changed when the
same forced eviction is followed by a different continuation policy.
Table~\ref{tab:linkage-continuation-summary} summarizes the linkage and
continuation-sensitivity evidence referenced throughout this section.

\textbf{Sampled MRU/random study.} The pre-registered sampled study
(\texttt{analysis/continuation\_\allowbreak policy\_\allowbreak sensitivity\_\allowbreak full\_\allowbreak 20260914/},
RUN\_ID \texttt{20260914T040333Z\_1f66342be435}) evaluates 5{,}000
primary decisions plus 500 diagnostic oversample decisions at capacities
32 and 128. Its prelaunch LRU-equivalence gate audited 1{,}271{,}616
candidate-horizon comparisons with 0 mismatches, and the run is validated
(\texttt{FULL\_EXPERIMENT\_VALID: true}). By the pre-registered Set-C rule
(median Jaccard $\geq 0.8$ and cross-continuation regret $\leq 1$ miss for
$\geq 80\%$ of LRU-optimal candidates), both MRU and mean-random
continuation classify ROBUST at this sampled scope. MRU is very close to
LRU: Set-C mean Jaccard is 0.9958 at capacity 32 and 0.9999 at capacity
128, with mean cross-continuation regret 0.0018 and 0.0001 misses,
respectively. Mean-random continuation also classifies ROBUST, but it is
visibly more perturbing: mean Jaccard is 0.7251 at capacity 32 and 0.8522
at capacity 128, with mean cross-continuation regret 0.0463 and 0.0197.

\textbf{Full-population MRU census.} The full-population MRU census
(\texttt{analysis/continuation\_\allowbreak policy\_\allowbreak mru\_\allowbreak population\_\allowbreak census\_\allowbreak 20260914/validated/20260914T042528Z\_\allowbreak 1a29e773a113/})
extends the MRU side of that result to all five families, capacities 32,
64, 128, and 256, and horizons 4, 8, and 16. The census validates 60/60
chunks and 2{,}363{,}286/2{,}363{,}286 decision-horizon records, with 0
duplicate, missing, or extra decision-horizon keys; 30/30 mandatory gates
and an independent recheck pass, and \texttt{FULL\_CENSUS\_VALID: true}.
Across all decision-horizon units, mean optimal-set Jaccard is
0.9994054593, median Jaccard is 1.0, 98.69\% of records have exact
Jaccard 1, mean cross-continuation regret is 0.000304471 misses, median
and p95 regret are both 0, the maximum mean regret is 0.193548 misses, and
99.9708\% of LRU-optimal candidates remain MRU-optimal on average. Strict
preference reversals are rare: 4{,}646 discordant candidate pairs out of
24{,}791{,}643{,}360 pairwise comparisons, a fraction of
$1.87\times10^{-7}$.

\textbf{Informative-stratum result.} Aggregate numbers alone would be
misleading because both-tied decisions are common: 67.31\% of all
decision-horizon records are all-tied under both LRU and MRU. The
pre-registered decisive stratum is therefore Set C, excluding decisions
already all-tied under LRU. In this informative stratum, the census has
764{,}282 decision-horizon units from 299{,}905 eviction decisions. Set-C
mean Jaccard is 0.9984587309, median Jaccard is 1.0, the strict reversal
fraction is $5.38\times10^{-7}$, mean cross-continuation regret is
0.000638213 misses, and the mean fraction of LRU-optimal candidates still
MRU-optimal is 0.9993955215. Since wiki2018 contributes no Set-C records,
the Set-C result is not inflated by the degenerate negative control; the
all-family and excluding-wiki Set-C summaries are identical.

\textbf{Capacity and horizon effects.} Sensitivity is largest at the
smallest capacity and longest horizon, but remains small in absolute
terms. By capacity in Set C, mean Jaccard and mean cross-continuation
regret are 0.994809/0.002067 at capacity 32, 0.998859/0.000530 at
capacity 64, 0.999806/0.000096 at capacity 128, and
0.999959/0.000019 at capacity 256. Thus the new population-only
capacities, 64 and 256, reveal no hidden sensitivity and cross no
pre-registered robustness threshold. By horizon in Set C, mean Jaccard
declines modestly from 0.999820 at $H=4$ to 0.999222 at $H=8$ and
0.996869 at $H=16$, while mean cross-continuation regret rises from
0.000039 to 0.000283 and 0.001355 misses. The direction is real but the
magnitude remains far below a practically meaningful miss-count change.

\textbf{Worst cell and scope.} The worst Set-C family/capacity/horizon cell
is alibaba-block/capacity 32/$H=16$: mean Jaccard 0.980433, median Jaccard
1.0, mean cross-continuation regret 0.003899 misses, and strict reversal
fraction $3.45\times10^{-5}$. Even this cell satisfies the
pre-registered robustness criterion. These results address the reviewer
concern within the evaluated scope: sampled MRU and random at capacities
32/128, and full-population MRU at capacities 32/64/128/256. They do not
establish population-scale random-continuation robustness, and they do not
show that every possible deployed continuation policy would preserve the
same ordering.
