\section{Continuation-Policy Sensitivity (RQ4)}
\label{sec:continuation}

The canonical labels (Section~\ref{sec:label-generation}) use LRU as the
continuation policy after a forced eviction. RQ4 asks how sensitive the
resulting labels are to that choice. This section reports \textbf{only the
validated sampled study}, at capacities 32 and 128. Source: repository

\textbf{Both alternative continuation policies classify ROBUST}, by the
pre-registered rule (median Jaccard $\geq 0.8$ and cross-continuation
regret $\leq 1$ miss for $\geq 80\%$ of LRU-optimal candidates, within the
discriminative-under-LRU stratum, Set C, $n=6597$), applied verbatim, not
re-derived after seeing results. \textbf{MRU continuation} is close to
exact agreement with LRU continuation at both capacities: median Jaccard
1.000, mean Jaccard 0.9958 (cap 32) / 0.9999 (cap 128), mean
cross-continuation regret 0.0018 / 0.0001 misses, strict reversal fraction
$2.7\times10^{-5}$ / $0.0$. \textbf{Mean-random continuation} (10 CRN
seeds) also classifies ROBUST but is visibly more perturbing: median
Jaccard remains 1.000 at both capacities, but \emph{mean} Jaccard is
noticeably lower (0.7251 at cap 32, 0.8522 at cap 128), mean
cross-continuation regret is an order of magnitude larger than MRU's
(0.0463 / 0.0197), and the strict reversal fraction, while still small, is
also an order of magnitude larger ($1.0\times10^{-3}$ / $1.5\times10^{-6}$).

\textbf{A horizon-dependent nuance that must not be smoothed over}: pooled
across both capacities, random continuation's median Jaccard is 1.000 at
$H=4$ and $H=8$ but drops to \textbf{0.881 at $H=16$} -- still above the
0.8 ROBUST threshold, but a real, measurable erosion of robustness as the
horizon lengthens, not a flat "always 1.0" result. MRU continuation's
median Jaccard stays at 1.000 across all three horizons. Reporting only
the pooled-by-capacity median (which happens to be 1.0 at both capacities
because it mixes horizons) would obscure this; both breakdowns are given
in Table~\ref{tab:linkage-continuation-summary} for exactly this reason.

\textbf{Scope discipline.} This robustness result covers capacities 32 and
128 only, and a stratified 5,000/500-decision sample, not the full
population. \textbf{Capacities 64 and 256 are not validated for
continuation sensitivity as of this writing.} A separate full-population
MRU continuation census is running to extend this result; its results are
UNVALIDATED and INCOMPLETE and are not used anywhere in this paper. Every
sentence in this section that reports a continuation-robustness number
should be read with the "sampled study, capacities 32/128" qualifier
attached, per the caveat discipline in
Section~\ref{sec:experimental-methodology}.
